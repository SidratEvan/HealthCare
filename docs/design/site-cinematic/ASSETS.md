# Campaign assets and provenance

Created 12 October 2026 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this
MedLiveBD redesign. These are synthetic illustrations, not commissioned photos,
actual people, actual hospital partners or documentary views. No real patient
reference images or data were supplied. No claim of model releases, exclusive
copyright or Creative Commons licensing is made for generated imagery.

The user's follow-up asked for more men and female doctors wearing hijab. Earlier
generations were superseded; only the four selected images above are used.

Uncompressed selected originals are preserved in `originals/`. Website renditions
are in `frontend/site/public/campaign`: 640, 1200 and 1920 pixel widths, AVIF and
WebP, generated by `frontend/site/scripts/prepare-campaign.mjs`. Only responsive
resizing, slight aspect-ratio crops and encoding were applied. No creative edits
were made outside the image-generation tool. `asset-sizes.json` records each file.
The hero requests an appropriately wide source on mobile for its tall crop.
Each image appears once on the page (refinement of 12 October 2026, see
`README.md`): `hero` in the hero, `team` in the hospital card, `patient` in
the patient card, `atmosphere` in the closing invitation.
Only the hero loads eagerly. Images are hosted locally with no external requests.

Public disclosure appears in the footer, bilingual image credits and privacy
notice. Original prompts established consistent slate-blue shadows, natural
warm skin, modest contemporary Bangladeshi clinical settings, unposed expressions,
no fabricated brands, no readable records and no distressing procedures.

## Geographic data

The existing `src/content/bangladesh-map.ts` geometry is preserved. This page uses
the country outline and eight division paths, not the legacy district layer.
Source: geoBoundaries gbOpen BGD ADM0 and ADM1, release `9469f09`, derived from
geoBoundaries / Wikimedia Commons. Licence: CC0 1.0. Verified against:

- https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/BGD/ADM0/
- https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/BGD/ADM1/
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The projection and simplification are the baseline's equirectangular geometry,
corrected for latitude. Decorative lines illustrate a future connection concept;
they do not identify hospitals, customers, active facilities or present coverage.
